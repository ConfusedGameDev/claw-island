## Description (both stores, max 4000)

Claw Island is a claw machine on tiny floating islands. Steer the crane, drop the claw, and fish out the prizes on your list. Miss and they roll away. Grab the wrong thing and you lose points. Get them all into the hatch and the rails stretch out to the next island.

This October it's Spooky Night. The crane is made of bones, the islands are haunted, and every island you clear hands you a body part from a random monster. A werewolf's head. A mummy's left arm. A slime's right leg. Clear all ten and your Frankenstein sits up and comes alive. Name it, spin it around in your hands, and send the picture to your friends. Nobody else will have the same one.

EVERY HATCH HAS A LOCK
Before anything goes down the hatch, you have to get it open, and every island does it differently:
• Ring the tombstone bells in the order they chimed
• Brew a potion with the right ingredients (the cauldron spits the wrong ones back at you)
• Balance the scale with exactly two pumpkins
• Carry the key to the padlock while Frankenstein stomps after you. He naps, eventually.
• Bounce a laser off the mirrors and into the crystal
• Or just find something heavy and drop it on the button

THINGS IN YOUR WAY
Bats that won't sit still. Ghosts. Ice where nothing stops sliding. A sandstorm that shoves the crane sideways. A conveyor belt that keeps feeding junk into the hatch. Gravity that only half works.

BOOSTER PACKS
Tear one open after each island: double points, double speed, or an instant catch that snaps onto whatever is under the claw.

A TUNE FOR EVERY ISLAND
The Monster Waltz plays on a music box in the pumpkin patch, on a creaky organ in the vampire castle and on a wobbly theremin in the mad scientist's lab.

CLASSIC ISLANDS
Had enough ghosts? The original ten islands are one tap away: a meadow full of cuccos with a grumpy guard, a beach where a diamond crab hides in the tide pool, a candy land where everything bounces, a factory, a volcano and a castle in the clouds. They have their own song too.

One more drop. Then bed.
