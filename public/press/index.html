In one line

A claw machine game on tiny floating islands where every haunted island you clear gives you a piece of your own Frankenstein.

Description

Claw Island is a claw machine on tiny floating islands. Steer the crane, drop the claw, and fish out the prizes on your list. Miss and they roll away. Grab the wrong thing and you lose points. Get them all into the hatch and the rails stretch out to the next island.

For Halloween it's Spooky Night. The crane is made of bones, the islands are haunted, and every island you clear hands you a body part from a random monster. Clear all ten and your Frankenstein comes alive. Name it, spin it around and share the picture. Nobody else will have the same one.

Each island locks its hatch in its own way: tombstone bells to ring back in order, a cauldron that wants a recipe, a pumpkin scale, a key carried past a stomping Frankenstein, lasers bounced off mirrors. Booster packs, a little tune for every island, and the original ten Classic islands round it out.

Features

  • 20 islands: 10 haunted Spooky Night islands and 10 Classic ones
  • Six kinds of hatch puzzles, mixed and matched per island
  • Build a Frankenstein from 10 random monster parts, view it in 3D and share it
  • Booster packs: double points, double speed, instant catch
  • Two original tunes, arranged differently for every island
  • Plays on phones, tablets and computers, with touch and keyboard controls
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
